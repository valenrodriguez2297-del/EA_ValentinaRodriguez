About

I spent the last year as the Executive Assistant to the founder of a yoga education platform serving two audiences: older adults building strength and mobility, and yoga teachers earning specialized certifications. What started as content execution — publishing videos, writing articles, designing social assets — grew into something bigger: I ended up owning the YouTube channel's growth, reviving an abandoned Pinterest account into the site's 4th-largest traffic source, and leading the mockups for a full homepage redesign.

Alongside the work, I completed a 12-week program in AI systems architecture for business and applied every module directly to a real company instead of a practice exercise. I don't just execute tasks — I build the prompt libraries, templates, and SOPs so the work doesn't depend on me being in the room. Native Spanish speaker, professional-level English as my daily working language, comfortable running async with teams spread across time zones.

Selected work

№4

Goal — turn a dead channel into real traffic

Reviving an abandoned Pinterest account into the site's biggest traffic source

The account had been inactive for a long time — duplicate profiles, and a large share of pins pointing to broken pages left over from a platform migration. I audited and rebuilt it from scratch: a production system for pins, reusable templates, and a prompt library that generates pin content straight from video transcripts. A steady cadence of 2–3 pins a week, tracked in Analytics, later handed off to a teammate to run independently. It became the 4th-largest traffic source to the site.

Goal — grow the channel without a viral bet

Running a YouTube channel's full pipeline, on a two-video-a-week cadence

Uploads, thumbnail design, A/B testing, descriptions, playlists, community posts — sustained for seven months with months scheduled fully in advance. Subscriber growth came out at 212% over the prior comparable period, with conversion per view roughly ten times higher and watch time nearly doubled. Over the tracked period the channel reached 34.8K views off 529.6K thumbnail impressions, holding a 3.0% click-through rate and a 3:08 average view duration. The driver was consistency, not a single viral hit.

Goal — stop losing people at login

Redesigning a homepage that was sending people to the wrong login

Two separate login systems — individual courses and annual membership — were generating a steady stream of confused support requests. I built and presented homepage and navigation mockups with one clear entry point, plus a fallback path for anyone who still landed in the wrong place. When I found the page builder couldn't safely duplicate the live homepage for testing, I paused and brought in the developer before touching production.

Goal — launch a newsletter that sounds like a person

Building a newsletter's entire identity, in the founder's voice

Logo and banners, an audio edition of every article, and a set of "Notes" written in first person — no rhetorical questions, no rounded numbers, sentence length that actually varies. I also resolved a copyright issue with the original logo along the way. The result: a fully-launched, professionally-branded newsletter with a voice framework the team could reuse.

How I work

The habits behind the results.

  • AI as a system, not a shortcut

    Reusable prompt libraries and AI projects instead of one-off prompts, so quality holds even when I'm not the one running it.

  • Documentation-first

    Guides and SOPs written so a teammate can pick up a task with zero back-and-forth.

  • Verify before delivering

    AI-generated drafts and summaries get checked against the source before they go out.

  • Flag risk before touching production

    If something could break a live site or campaign, that gets raised first — not fixed quietly after.

  • Async-ready

    A year of coordinating with teammates across time zones, with clear written handoffs.
